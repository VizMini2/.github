# Getting help

1. **Documentation**: <https://vizmini2.github.io/>. Most problems are covered in
   [Troubleshooting](https://vizmini2.github.io/help/troubleshooting.html),
   [Known limitations](https://vizmini2.github.io/help/limitations.html) and the
   [FAQ](https://vizmini2.github.io/help/faq.html).
2. **Bugs and ideas**: open an issue with the templates provided.
3. **Private matters** (account, privacy, data deletion): see
   [Contact and support](https://vizmini2.github.io/help/contact.html). From the
   app, *Home → Contact and bug reports* fills in the app version, Android version
   and phone model for you.
